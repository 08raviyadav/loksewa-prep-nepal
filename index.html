📖 How to study without getting bored

  • 20-minute blocks. Study in short bursts with a 5-minute break — use the Focus Timer above. Long sittings blur; short ones stick.
  • Mix topics. Don't grind one subject for hours — alternate Constitution, GK, Maths and IQ so your brain stays awake.
  • Repeat at intervals. Revisit a topic after 1 day, then 3 days, then a week — that's spaced repetition, and it beats cramming.
  • Test, don't just read. A 5-question Quick Quiz tells you more about what you know than re-reading a chapter.
  • End on a win. Finish each session with a topic you're good at — you'll come back tomorrow.

How practice works — and how you are scored

Every practice set shuffles both the question order and the option order, so no two attempts are the same. Hit 🔀 New random set for a fresh set any time.

Scoring is exactly like PSC: a correct 2-mark question = +2, a wrong one = −0.4 (20%), a skipped question = 0. A correct 1-mark question = +1, wrong = −0.2. Your result is judged against the real cutoff for your exam — Kharidar / NaSu 45, Section Officer 45, TSC 50, NRB Assistant 40.

Wrong answers are saved automatically to Bookmarks & Mistakes so you can revise them. XP, streaks, levels and badges keep it going — but the score is always honest.
